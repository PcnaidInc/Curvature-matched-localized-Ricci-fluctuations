1. **Analytic framework:** Published Hadamard/semiclassical and causal-response mathematics (cite appropriate sources). Adoption of formulas is not independently evaluated state data.
2. **Numerical component evidence:** R14's smooth-preparation enclosure and state-width-replacement allowance. Bound applies only under its explicitly defined model/library assumptions and to **one component**.

## Required controls
- Never replace a failed convergence result with a smoother plot or fitted counterterm; preserve original failures and changed settings.

- Both two-point/field legs, moving measurement/readout, initial terms, stress operator, local terms and frame/coordinate change contribute to the physical calculation; omission requires proof of vanishing.

- Do not claim original missing Batch93 scientific arrays were recovered; R93R14 is a separately executed bounded release. Do not revive rejected PCNBR-92 transfer.
- Controlled Schwarzschild/SL0-1 does not test a Kerr ring's quantum superposition, a classical/quantum hit/bypass ratio, or Hawking-origin negative-energy stress.
